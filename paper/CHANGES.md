## 2. Additions

* Subsection *Revised Controller in Simulation*: setup, three tables, two figures; the unfavourable results (larger whole-task
  maximum error, noisy force correlation in the benchmark, 1.6× vertical over-rendering, no delay robustness) are stated.
* Subsection *Controller Parameter Tuning* replaces the PD-toy figure (its "initial" curve had `K_p = K_d = 0`, i.e. no
  controller): it describes the `@param` workflow with the numbers from `docs/CONTROL_SIMULATOR_WALKTHROUGH.md`.
  Those numbers were produced with the Python mirror of `run_control_sim.m`; **rerun them in the MATLAB window before
  submission** (the text says so, delete that sentence when done).
* Table I is kept and relabelled "controller as submitted (hardware)".
* `references_additions.bib`: seven entries cited by the new text. **Verify each against the publisher**; page numbers and DOIs
  were written from memory.

## 3. Decisions for the authors

1. Admitting in the text that the submitted controller's lag came from the smoothing step and a zero MPC output (1259).
   It is accurate (see `docs/EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY.md`, §1), and a reviewer who reads the code will see it, but it is your call.
2. Whether the paper should present the revised controller at all before hardware numbers exist. The alternative is to keep the
   submitted controller as the result and move the revision to "future work"; the abstract and conclusion then need a one-line change.
3. `manipulator_teleop` (WebXR/Cartesian teleoperation) and `mink` are third-party code used by the repository: cite them in the
   Teleoperation Module and acknowledge in the README (done there).
4. The reviewer comment "infrastructure, not control" is answered by the equations above and by the simulation study; the
   force-path measurement on hardware is what would settle it.
5. Not changed because I could not judge it: all text outside the sections listed above, the figures `images/*.jpg|png`
   (not provided), and the bibliography file `references.bib` (not provided).
